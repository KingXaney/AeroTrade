# The owner's lab: `/learn` as a daily dashboard for computer vision, machine learning and quantitative finance

Status: In progress. Slices 1–5 built 2026-10-12 (the glossary's own address, the owner gate and
the beginner course's removal, the curriculum with its unit pages, the review cards, the daily
problem and the streak, the papers feed with its one model call a day); slice 7 follows (the
remaining twenty-one units, three a track per PR).

## Why

The owner wants to log in every day and learn current, advanced principles in three fields. The
beginner Learn hub (a 16-lesson course, Today, glossary and strategies tabs) was entry-level, so it
goes: for the owner's account `/learn` becomes the lab; for every other account the Learn section
keeps only the games, and the glossary — which the rest of the app links to — gets a page of its own.

## Decisions

- **Owner-only.** `LAB_OWNER_EMAILS` (comma-separated, compared lower-cased; unset means nobody)
  names the accounts that see the lab. `lib/lab/owner` is the one rule; the `(root)` layout
  computes the audience from the session and hands `navSectionsFor`/`navPagesFor` to every nav
  surface, so a non-owner's Learn section begins at the games and ⌘K offers them no Lab page.
- **The glossary at `/glossary`** for everyone (`components/learn/GlossaryGroups`, moved verbatim
  so every entry's id stays its key). Every producer of a `/learn#term` address — ⌘K, Today's
  lesson (`lessonLearnHref`), the daily email — now writes `/glossary#term`, and
  `components/learn/LearnRedirect` sends the old addresses there on the client, keeping the hash
  (a server redirect would drop it); with no hash, a non-owner goes to `/games`.
- **The course is removed for everyone** (registry, lesson pages, copy, the `courseDone` stamp,
  Home's course card, the landing syllabus, the robot's tip). Today's lesson, the first-week
  missions and the daily puzzle stay: they teach from the reader's own account, not a syllabus.
- **Four parts** of the lab to come: curated curriculum units authored into the repo (10 per
  track, the first 9 written to full depth, all 30 registered), a daily papers feed (arXiv cs.CV,
  cs.LG/stat.ML, q-fin.*, plus Hugging Face daily papers) fetched by a weekday 06:20 ET job,
  spaced-repetition review cards (an SM-2 variant, a daily streak like the games'), and one daily
  problem with a hidden worked solution. **One model call a day** (a cited digest of the papers,
  skipped when the Gemini daily quota is gone) and no other.
- **Copy rule.** The lab's chrome stays at the `'copy'` tier; unit bodies, cards and solutions will
  be held to a `'lab'` tier of trading-directed phrases only, since advanced ML and quant prose says
  "optimal" and "cheap to compute" as terms of art. The glossary stays the registry of the app's own
  metrics; units define their fields' vocabulary in their own bodies.

## The papers feed (slice 5)

- `lib/lab/papers/`: `config` (the categories each track lists and the wider set that places a
  paper, twelve a track, a week's novelty, the bounds), `links` (`paperLinks` from the arXiv id —
  the only place a paper URL is built), `arxiv` (the listing and id-list addresses,
  `parseArxivAtom` over fast-xml-parser with every entry read as data), `hf` (`parseHfDailyPapers`
  item by item with a loose zod object, `hfDatesFor`: the previous weekday and today), `day`
  (`mergePapers` by id — arXiv's categories, Hugging Face's upvotes, a Hugging Face paper with no
  arXiv row placed in machine learning — `isNovel`, `rankTrack`, `assembleDay`), `prompts` and
  `digest` (numbered papers, no ids or links; bullets kept only where they cite a shown paper and
  pass the 'lab' tier), `view` (`shapePapersView`: tracks in order, the digest's citations
  resolved to rows, `stale` when the day is not today), `store` (`saveLabDay` clears the digest,
  `saveLabDigest`, `readSeenIds`, `getLatestLabDay`) and `job` (the fetches, each returning its
  error rather than throwing).
- `database/models/lab-day.model.ts`: one document per ET day, no URL field, 45-day TTL.
- `lib/jobs/functions/lab.refreshLabPapers` — `lab-daily-papers`, weekdays 06:20 ET,
  `concurrency 1`: seen ids → Hugging Face (two days) → three arXiv listings three seconds apart
  → one id-list lookup → assemble (nothing new: skipped, yesterday stays the latest) → save →
  one `labDigest` call when a model is configured, a spent daily quota noted and the run kept.
  `npm run trigger -- lab` fires it locally. The status strip's row is "Lab papers".
- The page: `?view=papers` (`components/lab/PapersPanel` per field, `DigestPanel` with "[n]"
  links to the rows, a stale note, an empty state before the first run); Today carries the digest
  with a link to the view, or three papers a field when there is no digest.
- `AI_PLAN` and `GEMINI_API_KEY_PAID` (`lib/ai/models.geminiApiKey`, read by the Inngest client
  and the chat route): the owner enables Gemini billing on a project and names that project's key
  as the paid one; flipping the plan on the deployment moves every call between the free key and
  the paid one without a code change, and a paid plan without its key falls back to the free key.
- Tests: the readers on fixtures pinned from one real fetch of each feed on 2026-10-12, the day
  assembly (no `http` in a stored day), the digest parser and its prompt at the advice tier, the
  view; `qa-lab` seeds a `labdays` document and checks the Papers view, Today's digest, the stale
  note and the empty state.
