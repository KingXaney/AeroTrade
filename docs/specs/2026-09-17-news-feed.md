# Personal news feed

**Status:** implemented on `feat/news-feed` (2026-09-17).

## Why

Every general-news surface was hardwired to one input, the watchlist: the dashboard news
widget, the `/history` news column and the chat's `getMarketNews` all called Finnhub company
news for watchlist symbols, or Finnhub's US-market wire when the watchlist was empty. Nothing
let a user say what news they wanted, and a new user with no watchlist got a US equities wire
rather than the day's biggest stories.

## What

A per-user **news feed preference** that drives four surfaces — the new `/news` page, the
dashboard `market-news` widget (now "News feed"), `/history`, and the daily digest email —
with four controls and one default.

| | |
|---|---|
| Default | Google News top stories, US edition (`https://news.google.com/rss?hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en`): the biggest stories, every subject, ranked by prominence |
| Categories | Top stories · World · National · Business · Technology · Science · Health · Sports · Entertainment (Google topic sections) · Markets (the CNBC / MarketWatch / Yahoo Finance wires the digest already reads); up to 6 |
| Regions | US, GB, CA, AU, IN, SG, IE, NZ, ZA — English Google News editions, up to 4; every category is fetched per region |
| Outlets | Preferred (only these are shown) and hidden, up to 12 each, matched by identity so `Reuters`, `reuters.com` and `https://www.reuters.com/` are one outlet |
| Keywords | Up to 8, normalised like topic keywords, fetched as one Google News search |
| Watchlist | Optional, off by default: Finnhub company news for watchlist symbols mixed in |

## Where things live

- `lib/news/feed-prefs.ts` — **client-safe** pure module: the type, the tables, the caps, the
  zod schema, `normalizeNewsFeed` (lenient: unknown ids dropped, lists deduped and capped,
  a feed can never come out empty), `outletKey`, `describeNewsFeed`, `planFeedSlots`.
- `lib/news/feed.ts` — server-side pure module (re-exports the above): `feedRequestsFor`
  (preference → Google News URLs), `filterBySources`, `mergeFeed`, `pickDigestArticles`.
- `lib/news/adapters/search.ts` — `fetchGoogleNewsFeed(url)` for any Google News RSS URL
  (search, front page, topic sections; the 302 on sections is followed), under the single
  `NEWS_SEARCH_ENABLED` kill switch. `fetchNewsForQuery` is a thin wrapper.
- `lib/news/feed-store.ts` — server-only reads: `getNewsFeedPrefs`, `getNewsFeedForPrefs`,
  `getNewsFeed`. Every request settles on its own; with Google off or empty the feed
  degrades to the market wires plus Finnhub and reports `fallback: true`.
- `lib/actions/news-feed.actions.ts` — `saveNewsFeed`, `resetNewsFeed`.
- `database/models/user-preferences.model.ts` — `newsFeed`, a single-nested sub-schema with
  no defaults. **Absence is the default**; saving the default unsets the field.
- `lib/preferences/upsert.ts` — the upsert-with-retry and `$unset` every preference action
  shares (a plain module: a `'use server'` export taking a userId would be an
  unauthenticated write endpoint).
- `app/(root)/news/` · `components/news/NewsFeedEditor.tsx` · `components/news/NewsArticleCard.tsx`
  · `components/settings/NewsFeedSettings.tsx`.

## The ranking rule

Batches arrive in request priority: the user's keywords, then each category in canonical
order across each region (front page before any section), then the markets wires, then the
watchlist. `mergeFeed` takes one unseen article from each batch in turn until the limit, so
a 70-item section can never swamp the 38-item front page and every chosen feed gets a bounded
share. Within a batch the fetcher's order stands: Google's editorial prominence for the front
page and sections — the rank is a cross-outlet signal a time sort would throw away — and
newest-first for search and wires. Dedupe keys on the URL with its **case preserved**
(Google's article ids are case-sensitive base64url; `normalizeUrl` lowercases and could merge
two stories) or on a normalised headline.

## Bounds

At most `MAX_FEED_REQUESTS = 8` network calls per page view, truncated in `planFeedSlots`
(the editor says how many feeds fell off). Every Google URL is cached for
`FEED_REVALIDATE_SECONDS` (15 minutes) per URL, so the shared default is one fetch per server
per quarter hour. User text reaches a URL only inside `encodeURIComponent`, and
`fetchGoogleNewsFeed` refuses any URL outside the Google News base.

## The digest

`sendDailyNewsSummary` folds up to `FEED_DIGEST_CAP = 5` feed articles in after the market
pool, under `TOTAL_ARTICLE_CAP`. The prompt routes `sourceType 'web'` into one
"🗞️ Top Headlines" section with no forced market angle. `sanitizeDigestHtml` compares
`normalizeUrl` on both sides, so the Google redirect links pass its allowlist. Since the
default feed is never empty, digests now send for users the old pipeline skipped for lack of
a Finnhub key.

## Verification

Unit: `lib/news/__tests__/feed.test.ts` (normalisation, schema, equality, description,
planning, URLs, outlet identity, source filter, merge, digest pick), plus additions to the
search and utils suites. Browser: `scripts/qa/qa-news-feed.mjs` — default stored as absence,
every control persisting through a real save, reload and Mongo read, a hidden outlet gone from
`/news` and `/history`, reset from settings, save-default-equals-reset.

**Trap found while verifying:** a long-running `next dev` keeps Mongoose's compiled model, so
a schema change made after it started is silently dropped by strict mode on update. Restart
the dev server after touching a model.

## Out of scope

A chat `getNewsFeed` tool · non-English editions · outlet matching beyond the documented
key normaliser (`latimes.com` vs "Los Angeles Times") · applying the feed to the stock page ·
Crypto and Politics as first-class categories (Google has no such sections; keywords cover
crypto, National covers domestic politics).
